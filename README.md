## Features

### Every store, one library

Your last games up top, everything else sorted the way you like. Steam, Game Pass, Epic, GOG, EA, Ubisoft and more, side by side.

<img src="docs/img/home-tiles.jpg" width="100%" alt="Arcade Launcher home screen with Continue row and game library">

### Know what to play

Trailers, How Long To Beat times, achievements, playtime and play status for every game.

<img src="docs/img/game-details.jpg" width="100%" alt="Game page with How Long To Beat times, achievements and play status">

### A Home button that goes home

Press Home once to come back to Arcade from any game. Press it twice to see everything that is running and switch, like on a phone.

<img src="docs/img/open-now.jpg" width="100%" alt="Open now app switcher opened with the handheld Home button">

### Quick settings, one press away

Volume, brightness, power mode, refresh rate, Wi-Fi and sleep without leaving the couch.

### Tiles, icons or console row

Pick the layout that suits your screen. Dark, light or OLED black, in English or Arabic.

### Edit anything with the controller

Rename, hide, change cover art, build collections, set per-game profiles or uninstall, all from one menu.

## Install

1. **[Download the latest version](https://github.com/AMSRS/Arcade-Launcher/releases/latest)**: `ArcadeLauncher-Setup` (installer) or `ArcadeLauncher-Portable` (zip).
2. Open it. A one-minute setup asks for your language, look, game folders and apps.
3. Turn on **Start with Windows** so your handheld boots straight into Arcade.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", select **More info › Run anyway** (the app isn't code-signed yet).

Arcade Launcher is free. **Arcade Plus** is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks a few extras and keeps development going: the console row with moving backgrounds, accent colors and the OLED theme, your own wallpapers and music, per-game profiles, battery estimates, Steam friends and moving your setup to another PC. Get it in the app: **☰ › Get Arcade Plus**.

## FAQ

<details><summary><b>Is it free?</b></summary><br>Yes, on as many PCs as you like. Arcade Plus adds optional extras for a one-time price.</details>
<details><summary><b>Is it a Playnite or Steam Big Picture alternative?</b></summary><br>Yes. It's a full-screen, controller-first library for all your games, and it's also made to replace the Windows desktop on a handheld: file manager, Home button app switcher, quick settings and per-game power profiles, set up in a minute with no plugins.</details>
<details><summary><b>Does it work with Xbox Game Pass, Epic and GOG?</b></summary><br>Yes. Games from the Xbox app (PC Game Pass), Epic, GOG Galaxy, EA app, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net, itch.io and RetroArch are found automatically, without logging in.</details>
<details><summary><b>Can my ROG Ally or Legion Go boot straight into it?</b></summary><br>Yes. Turn on Start with Windows. With the Home button option on, Home takes you back to Arcade instead of the desktop.</details>
<details><summary><b>Will it slow down my games?</b></summary><br>No. Arcade sits idle while a game runs. Moving backgrounds and Steam friends are off unless you turn them on.</details>
<details><summary><b>Can I add emulators?</b></summary><br>Yes. RetroArch playlists are picked up automatically, and any emulator (PCSX2, Dolphin, DuckStation, PPSSPP…) can be added with its ROM folder.</details>
<details><summary><b>Where are my settings? Can I move them to another handheld?</b></summary><br>In <code>%LOCALAPPDATA%\Arcade</code>. With Arcade Plus, Settings › Back up Arcade setup saves everything to a zip you can restore on another PC.</details>
<details><summary><b>Is it safe?</b></summary><br>Arcade collects no data, shows no ads and needs no account. It goes online only for game art and info (Steam, HowLongToBeat, PCGamingWiki), wallpapers (Bing), update checks (GitHub) and Plus license checks.</details>
