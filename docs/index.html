Your handheld, minus the desktop.

Arcade Launcher is a free home screen for Windows handhelds. It finds your games from every store and puts them in one place you can drive with a controller, so your ROG Ally, Legion Go or MSI Claw feels like a console from the moment it turns on.

One library instead of six launchers

Every game sits on one screen with its cover art, sorted by what you played last. Apps and folders live there too, so you rarely need the Windows desktop.

Pick up where you left off

The games you played most recently sit at the top. Everything else sorts itself the way you like.

  • Sort by last played, most played, A to Z, newest or shortest to beat
  • Filter by status, store or your own collections
  • Rename, hide or change the cover of any game with the controller

Know what you're getting into

Open a game to see its trailer and everything that helps you decide what to play tonight.

  • How Long To Beat times for the story, extras and 100%
  • Achievements, your playtime and play status
  • Install size and space left on the drive

A Home button that goes home

Press your handheld's Home button to come back to Arcade from any game. Press it twice to see everything that's running and jump between apps, like on a phone.

  • No desktop flashing between games
  • Arcade comes straight back when you quit a game
  • Quick settings for volume, brightness, power mode and refresh rate

Big tiles, small icons or a console row

Choose the layout that suits your screen and your library. Dark, light and English or Arabic.

  • Small icons show a big library at a glance
  • The console row puts one game's artwork across the whole screen
  • Rotating wallpapers and quiet background music

Questions

Is Arcade Launcher free?

Yes. Download it and use it on as many PCs as you like. Arcade Plus adds optional extras for a one-time price.

Is it a Playnite or Steam Big Picture alternative?

Yes. It's a full-screen, controller-first library for all your games, and it's also made to replace the Windows desktop on a handheld, with a file manager, a Home button app switcher, quick settings and per-game power profiles. Setup takes about a minute and needs no plugins.

Does it work with Xbox Game Pass, Epic and GOG?

Yes. Games from Steam, the Xbox app (PC Game Pass), Epic Games Store, GOG Galaxy, EA app, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net, itch.io and RetroArch are found automatically, without logging in.

Can my ROG Ally or Legion Go start straight into Arcade?

Yes. Turn on Start with Windows during setup. With the Home button option on, pressing Home brings you back to Arcade instead of the desktop.

Will it slow down my games?

No. Arcade sits idle while you play. Moving backgrounds and Steam friends stay off unless you turn them on.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". What do I do?

The app isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns the first time you open it. Select More info, then Run anyway. Arcade collects no data, shows no ads and needs no account.
